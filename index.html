Digital Lab Equipment Booking

A simple way for students and researchers to book shared laboratory equipment without clashes.

Shared lab equipment like microscopes and centrifuges is used by many people. When two people try to use the same machine at the same time, work gets delayed. This system lets you check what is free, request a slot, and track your bookings in one place.

How it works

1. Choose equipment

Open the catalogue and pick the equipment you need. Equipment under maintenance cannot be booked.

2. Pick a free slot

Use the availability page to find a green slot, then fill the booking form with your date and time.

3. Wait for approval

The lab admin approves or rejects the request. You will get a message on the notifications page.
